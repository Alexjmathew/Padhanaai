Why does SGD use mini-batches instead of the full dataset?

According to your document, SGD uses mini-batches for two core architectural advantages:

  • Computational efficiency: Reduces per-step cost from O(N) over entire dataset to O(B) per mini-batch.
  • Optimization dynamics: The stochastic noise naturally helps the model escape high-dimensional saddle points.
