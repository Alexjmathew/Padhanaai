Why does SGD use mini-batches instead of the full dataset?

According to your document, SGD uses mini-batches for two core advantages:

  • Computational efficiency: Reduces per-step cost from $\mathcal{O}(N)$ to $\mathcal{O}(B)$.
  • Optimization dynamics: Mini-batch noise helps the optimizer navigate past saddle points.
